Foundations for research agents

MMLU-Pro · NeurIPS 2024

Can a benchmark distinguish stronger reasoners?

MMLU-Pro includes 12,032 questions across 14 disciplines, with up to ten plausible options per question. Asking GPT-4o to work through a problem before answering makes a larger difference on this benchmark.

PaperDataset

Critique Fine-Tuning · COLM & EMNLP 2025

What can a student learn from a teacher’s critique?

An answer key reveals an error. A critique can locate the step that caused it. CFT trains a student to generate a teacher’s analysis of a candidate answer. On new math problems, the student answers directly.

One-Shot CFT narrows the training set to one seed problem, using 600 filtered solution–critique examples per seed. Whether critique can guide a live research decision remains an open question.

ScholarCopilot · COLM 2025

Which paper belongs at this citation?

Scientific writing brings in evidence beyond a model’s own reasoning. ScholarCopilot emits a retrieval token while drafting, uses its context to retrieve a paper, and conditions the next passage on that paper.

ReproBridge · 2026

Can a fresh session reproduce the measurement?

Even with a source in hand, recovering a result can take days of work. ReproBridge preserves the recovered code, environment, launchers, and guide so the next researcher can rerun the experiment.

In ScientistTwo, we applied this approach to reproducible input repositories and generated code.

Proposed research · independent audit

What should the agent check next?

A rerun can reproduce a number even when the comparison uses different test splits. I propose linking each claim to its evidence, then testing whether an independent auditor can identify what to check while there is still time to act.

Study A · Fixed episodes

Which records make a claim assessable?

Hold the claim and completed experiment fixed. Vary the evidence an auditor can inspect, from the final paper and repository to linked configurations and raw outputs.

Measure Unsupported acceptance at matched coverage.

Study B · Live episodes

Does feedback improve the next decision?

Compare passive audit, fixed checks, and adaptive feedback under equal model and execution budgets. Follow the claim through the next inspection, rerun, or revision.

Measure Fraction of assigned episodes that reach an independently supported conclusion, including well-founded negative results.
